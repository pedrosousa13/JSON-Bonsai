# Privacy Policy — JSON Bonsai

JSON Bonsai does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

- All processing happens locally in your browser.
- The extension reads page content only to detect and render JSON documents; that content never leaves your machine.
- The "storage" permission is used solely to save your preferences on your own device. Query/search history stays local and can be disabled in Settings; `window.data` exposure is off by default and stored only when you enable it.
- No analytics, no tracking, no remote servers.

Questions: https://github.com/pedrosousa13/JSON-Bonsai/issues
